### q3 (multi_chunk) -- How does agentic AI differ from traditional generative AI or chatbots?

**Key differences between agentic AI and traditional generative AI/chatbots**

- **Goal‑driven autonomy** – Agentic AI is designed to pursue explicit goals and can act independently to achieve them, whereas generative AI/chatbots mainly produce text in response to user prompts without a broader objective. [3]  
- **Reactivity + Proactivity** – Agentic AI can sense changes in its environment and react, but it also takes initiative based on its goals (proactive behavior). Traditional chatbots are largely reactive, waiting for user input before responding. [1]  
- **Continuous learning and adaptation** – Agentic AI learns continuously and adapts its actions to maintain progress toward its goals, while conventional chatbots rely on static models or rule sets that do not evolve on their own. [3]  
- **BDI architecture** – Agentic AI follows the Belief‑Desire‑Intention (BDI) model, giving it internal states (beliefs, desires, intentions) that guide planning and execution. Generative AI lacks this internal decision‑making structure. [2]  
- **Self‑improvement capability** – When an agentic AI fails to meet performance parameters, it can autonomously adjust its behavior; traditional generative AI does not self‑modify during operation. [1]
